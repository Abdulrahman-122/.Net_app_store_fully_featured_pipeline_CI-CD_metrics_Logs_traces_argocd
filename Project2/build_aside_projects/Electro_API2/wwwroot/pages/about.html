About ElectroStore

ElectroStore is a clean, modern electronics storefront built for fast browsing, simple cart management, and easy admin control. It connects to a backend API, preserves dark/light theme preference, and gives users a polished shopping experience across every device.

What this app delivers

From product discovery to checkout, ElectroStore is designed to feel smooth and reliable. The application includes a responsive product catalog, cart management, product details, admin dashboard, and route-based navigation for pages like About and Contact.

Fast product browsing

Explore products with clear pricing, stock status, and a polished layout that keeps every item easy to scan.

Smart cart experience

Quantity controls, subtotals, and a totals summary make shopping intuitive and predictable.

Admin controls

Manage products, orders, customers, and categories from a dedicated admin dashboard.

Dark mode support

Toggle between dark and light themes with persistent settings stored in browser memory.

Modern interface

A refined visual system with glowing accents, glass-like cards, and responsive layouts for phones and desktops.

API-powered

Uses a backend API for product data while keeping frontend pages self-contained and easy to maintain.

How it works

1

Browse products

Open the shop page and explore the catalog with product cards that show images, names, and prices.

2

View details

Open a product page to see full specifications, stock status, and add items to your cart.

3

Contact support

Use the Contact page to reach the admin for help, questions, or feedback about the application.
